Know Your Risk

Patterns We Watch For

A board is only as useful as the judgment behind it. These are the categories of risk our analysts are trained to recognize and translate into action.

Protests Near Corporate Offices

Demonstrations, planned or spontaneous, that affect routing, building access, or principal visibility near a worksite.

Insider Access Risk

Tailgating, credential sharing, and other gaps that let unauthorized people into controlled spaces.

Elicitation Attempts

Casual-seeming conversations designed to draw out schedules, travel plans, or household details.

Deepfake Impersonation

Synthetic audio or video used to impersonate a principal or executive for fraud or reputational harm.

Household Exposure

Risk that follows a principal home — family routines, residence details, and household staff access.

Cyber-to-Physical Pivot

A digital compromise — email, calendar, smart-home access — used to enable a physical-world threat.

Suspicious Mail & Packages

Unscheduled deliveries or packages that warrant screening before they reach a principal or office.

Trigger Events & Anniversaries

Dates, announcements, or events that are statistically more likely to coincide with elevated risk.
